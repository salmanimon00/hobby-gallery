This dated package consolidates the Morning, Evening, Afternoon, Weekend Self-Care, and Healthy Eating Pinterest planning material. It is designed for quick handoff to a designer, content editor, or publisher.

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|

| `02-visual-templates` | Reusable image templates, hosted-asset links, and visual rules. |
