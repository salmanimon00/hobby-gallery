## Cover

**CoreWellFit Pinterest Pin Workflow**

From checklist idea to organized, post-ready package

## Slide 1

### One workflow, repeatable across every Pin series

- Start with a specific category, Board, and Board section.
- Group titles into a numbered Pin sequence.
- Match each title to a consistent checklist template.
- Prepare descriptions, alternative text, asset links, and publishing status before handoff.

## Slide 2

### The package is organized for fast team handoff

- `01-content-plan` holds titles, ranges, and Board/section assignments.
- `02-visual-templates` holds design rules and managed graphic links.
- `03-pin-metadata` holds descriptions, alternative text, and readiness status.
- `04-publishing` holds planned dates, live URLs, and status updates.

## Slide 3

### Taxonomy keeps related Pins discoverable

- **Health & Fitness:** Morning, Evening, Afternoon, Weekend Self-Care, sleep, hydration, and routines.
- **Food and Drink:** Healthy Meals, Meal Planning, and Nutrition Habits.
- Boards provide the collection; sections create practical content clusters.
- Pin IDs preserve the intended content sequence from draft through publication.

## Slide 4

### The visual system stays calm, readable, and recognizably CoreWellFit

- White dominates the canvas; Navy anchors the headline; Green highlights only key actions.
- Every template uses a 2:3 portrait Pinterest format and a mobile-first text hierarchy.
- Natural-light still life photography supports the routine topic without visual clutter.
- One headline, one useful message, short checklist cues, and a quiet CoreWellFit.com footer.

## Slide 5

### Four reusable checklist templates cover the routine library

- **Morning checklist:** water, breakfast, planner, or sneakers in natural daylight.
- **Busy morning checklist:** practical life-admin still life for parents and professionals.
- **Healthy evening checklist:** tea, book, linen, and a bright calming wind-down scene.
- **Bedtime checklist:** book, water, sleep mask, and a simple 9 PM bedside arrangement.

## Slide 6

### Metadata makes each Pin ready to publish later

- Each record stores the Pin ID, title, Board/section, description, alternative text, and assigned template.
- Descriptions emphasize useful, practical habit support without medical or guaranteed-outcome claims.
- Alternative text describes the actual visual and headline for accessible use.
- The publishing log captures planned date, published date, live URL, status, and notes.

## Slide 7

### Healthy Eating Pins 31–45 now extend the system

- Fifteen new titles cover meal prep, grocery planning, breakfast, snacks, hydration, and kitchen resets.
- Ten JPG graphics are in managed asset generation and recorded in the visual manifest.
- Pins 41–45 have complete metadata and visual directions; JPG generation is queued until the image quota resets.
- The full series remains inside the same dated package and Board structure.

## Slide 8

### Team action: review, publish, and keep the system current

- Review title fit, visual readability, and Board placement before publishing.
- Use the managed asset link from the graphic manifest rather than copying large files into the code repository.
- Mark each Pin as Draft, Ready for Review, Scheduled, or Published in the publishing log.
- Add new themes by repeating the same folder, metadata, and template sequence.
