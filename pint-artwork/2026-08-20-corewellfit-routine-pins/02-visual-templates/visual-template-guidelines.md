# CoreWellFit Routine Checklist Pin Templates

## Shared system

Use a **2:3 vertical Pinterest canvas** with a clean white field, Deep Navy `#2C3E50` for headline hierarchy, and Green `#3CB371` only for checkmarks, rules, or a single emphasis shape. Keep the layout editorial and calm: the title dominates, supporting text stays brief, and the CoreWellFit.com footer remains quiet.

> Each graphic should communicate one useful routine idea in a format that feels printable, readable on mobile, and easy to save.

## Template A — Morning checklist

Place a two- or three-line navy title in the upper third. Use a bright natural-light still life—water, breakfast, sneakers, notebook, or a clear counter—in the lower half. Add up to five checkmarks in a slim vertical column and a single green underline below the title. Use for Pin IDs 1–7 and 9–15.

## Template B — Busy morning checklist

Use the same color system with a practical life-admin still life: tote, water bottle, sneakers, breakfast item, or planner. Keep the visual warm but uncluttered. Use for Pin ID 8 and related parent or professional morning content.

## Template C — Healthy evening checklist

Use a high-key evening still life: tea, book, soft linen, or a bedside lamp. Keep the overall canvas light, with Navy for title and one Green accent rule. Use for Pin IDs 16–21 and 25–27.

## Template D — Bedtime checklist

Use a calm bedside arrangement: closed book, water glass, sleep mask, and a simple clock. Use generous white space and no more than five checklist marks. Use for Pin IDs 22–24 and 28–30.

## Accessibility and copy limits

Maintain high contrast, do not rely only on color for checklist status, and keep graphics to one clear headline plus a short supporting line. Avoid medical promises, extreme weight-loss framing, and dense text blocks.
